Frequently Asked Questions

Does Subtitle2short upload my subtitle?

Subtitle parsing and processing are performed locally in your browser.

What does the animated preview show?

It follows the generated subtitle timestamps and changes the caption whenever the next generated frame begins.

Can I preview faster than real time?

Yes. Use 1× for normal timing and 2×, 4×, or 8× to inspect short-form captions quickly.

About Subtitle2short

Subtitle2short is a browser-based utility for transforming standard subtitle cues into shorter caption frames.

It is designed for short-form video workflows where captions may need to change rapidly and remain easy to read on vertical video.

The animated preview is a visual simulation of the generated SRT timing. It is not a video renderer.
